# Complex Portal direct domain access

Version: v1.1
Date: 2026-10-14
Status: current

`ComplexPortalDatabase.from_files(complextab=..., release_version=...)` consumes
one caller-supplied official curated ComplexTab file. It never downloads or
infers release identity from filenames. `complexes()` retains the unchanged
19 official source headers and their order. The source is small (the fixed
2026-01-09 human file is about 5 MB), so validation may collect identifiers and
invalid-row predicates. Returned relations use the shared replayable source boundary
over native CSV batches, with projection, predicate and row-limit pushdown. File identity
is checked at plan creation, every execution, before emitting each batch and after the
scan, so an existing lazy plan cannot silently consume a replaced source.

`expanded_members()` expands the official Expanded participant list, which
already resolves nested complex participants. It does not infer members from
complex-to-pathway relationships or recursively substitute missing complexes.
UniProt isoform and processed-chain suffixes remain exact; stoichiometry zero
means unknown and is represented by a null numeric value plus its original token.
Alternative sets in square brackets expand to separate candidate rows sharing
one member_group_id; they are not simultaneous subunits. Non-UniProt participants in the primary participant list are not protein members.
Taxon and confidence are complex facts, not an assertion that every component
belongs to the complex taxon (host/pathogen complexes can mix component taxa).

Missing required headers/identifiers, invalid complex/taxon/member tokens,
empty files, duplicate complex IDs and duplicate expanded participant identities fail.
UniProt base accessions follow the official [6/10-character grammar](https://www.uniprot.org/help/accession_numbers),
with exact isoform/processed-chain suffixes retained. Participant uniqueness is per
complex after alternative expansion, including repeated alternatives and conflicting
stoichiometries. Numeric stoichiometry must fit the exposed nonnegative Int64 type;
overflow fails during construction, before any public relation is returned.
`with_taxon()` filters the validated source; absent taxon yields typed empty
relations. Source mutation after construction fails at relation creation or execution.
Relations record release identity and original member tokens. No analysis,
stoichiometry weighting, gene aggregation, or acquisition policy belongs here.

The expanded participant list is documented in the official ComplexTab release
README and [Complex Portal 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8689886/).
Curated and predicted ComplexTab are separate source products; this adapter's
caller must supply the curated product and retain its acquisition provenance.

The fixed 2026-01-09 official E. coli file includes one expanded IntAct identifier,
`EBI-25775462`, in CPX-4382. Preserve it as namespace `intact`; do not turn it
into a UniProt accession or silently drop the member. The expanded relation
therefore uses `participant_id` plus `identifier_namespace`.
